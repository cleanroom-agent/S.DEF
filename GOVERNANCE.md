# Governance

## Overview

S.DEF is an open specification governed by its community of contributors and maintainers.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** — Responsible for the specification's direction and approving proposals
- **Contributors** — Anyone who contributes code, documentation, or proposals
- **Community** — Users and stakeholders who provide feedback

## Decision Making

Decisions are made through a consensus-seeking process. For major changes, the proposal process is used.
